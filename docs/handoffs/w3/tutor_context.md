# Separate tutor context

This handoff supports a separate tutoring conversation. It does not assert
lesson completion, change the learning course or require a lesson or quiz
before approved engineering work.

Assume introductory physics, single-variable calculus and basic Python.
Snell's law relates angles measured from the surface normal. A closed body
requires finding the nearest finite face: crossing an infinite supporting
plane alone does not prove that a ray entered glass. Refraction changes the
medium only at a certified crossing; total internal reflection keeps the
incident medium.

The implementation follows directions and ideal bookkeeping weights. It does
not calculate Fresnel power, polarization, dispersion or a coherent wave field.
The visible path thickness and transparency are display aids. Interval bounds
represent arithmetic uncertainty; an unresolved critical or grazing case must
report failure rather than choose the visually preferred route.

The parallel block, prism deviation and internal-TIR presets connect these
ideas to inspected events. Their independent references and limitations are
documented in `math_used.md` and `tests_and_evidence.md`; a plausible picture
alone is not numerical validation.
