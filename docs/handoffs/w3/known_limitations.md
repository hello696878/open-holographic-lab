# W3 known limitations

This is direction-only, constant-index geometrical routing. It is not a
calibrated power, polarization, dispersion, side-scattering or W1 field model.
The visible statement remains: **Ideal route weights; Fresnel losses are not
modeled.** Path colors identify medium; line thickness, transparency and mounts
are display aids, not measured width, physical power or optical surfaces.

The model supports one external source and at most one closed convex block or
right triangular prism. All side/end faces are optical boundaries. Only the
explicitly admitted W2 exterior elements may coexist. Sufficient separating
halfspaces can reject some physically separated corner arrangements. Touching,
embedded, ambiguous or unresolved critical/grazing/edge configurations fail
closed; input is never moved or normalized to manufacture a route.

The shipped original alternating-interface algebraic fixture stops at operation
14: direction component width 1.474560851200124e-8 exceeds the unchanged 1e-8
bound. The historical planning diagnostic continued to an interval division
across zero at 32; that history remains untouched. This chain is not a
realizable 32-event single-body trajectory. Neither that observation nor a
small nominal norm residual proves a universal event limit. A separate actual
body-scene interaction cap remains a resource boundary. Successful declared
closed-body trajectories and independently checked enclosures are qualified in
the evidence handoff.

Body-present limits are smaller than W2's, and do not reduce W2 or the no-body
delegation. Resource truncation preserves every unresolved terminal weight and
medium; numerical failure is not an incomplete successful result. Exact
stored-input/affine targets differ conceptually from ideal mathematical angles.
Measured residuals are not universal accuracy guarantees.

The 16/32 MiB global and 4/8 MiB W3 ownership budgets cover declared app-owned
records, buffers, DOM and geometry reservations. Python tracemalloc excludes
untraced native allocations; browser app ownership is not total V8/browser/GPU
or process RSS. Measured cases cannot prove every permitted numeric spelling;
the hard bounded encoder and admission checks remain authoritative.

Floating component labels can overlap in prism views. The component list and
selected-event Inspector remain readable; complete numeric strings use local
scrolling. The 42.56-second original recording includes a stationary TIR hold
in its second half. Media QA inspected ten genuine screenshots and eight
five-second-spaced video samples, rather than claiming continuous manual
playback. Headless Chrome RAF observations are not a guaranteed display frame
rate or a GPU timing measurement.

There is no persistence, curved/nested/overlapping/imported body, material
database, source bundle, Fresnel branch, optical path/phase/group delay, wave
coupling, hardware or deployment. Original settings/bundle contracts and
historical W1/W2 evidence remain unchanged. Visual acceptance and publication
are separate from the implementation candidate; no commit or push is authorized.
