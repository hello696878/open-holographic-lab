# Limits of A's independent theoretical model

- This is the declared probability-mean second-moment observation of256 local
  coherent Jones samples. A static random phase mask does not prove a physical
  detector discards all cross terms. Spatial coherence, propagation followed by
  finite-aperture collection and actual instrumentation need another model.
- Gaussian phase mean/spread are radians and spread is standard deviation.
  Saved experimental `dphi`, DOP and S-channel mappings remain unresolved.
  No `/100`,π or2π data conversion is imported from the paper. A's default
  M256 and waveplate angles are model choices, not recovered historical settings.
- The finite submitted ensemble is authoritative. The separately labelled
  infinite-population prediction is not an error bar or a replacement result.
  No confidence interval is invented from one realization. The test-only
  uniform population check does not add a product distribution or sweep.
- Mean S0/C/Stokes use amplitude-unit squared, not calibrated watts or a
  physical-area integral. Full Stokes, normalized Stokes and polarized direction
  have different denominators. Exactdark DoP/normalized values are null.
  Accepted arithmetic-domain residuals are flagged and left raw; arbitrary
  nonphysical matrices are rejected rather than projected/clipped.
- Same-platform installed-library reproducibility is tested; cross-version RNG
  and cross-platform bitwise equality are not promised. Actual unwrapped and
  periodic phases are preserved alongside the seed and complete settings.
- Mounted geometry, reference planes and visible light-path guides are a
  functional schematic. Unknown historical distances, cube type/splitting and
  reflection frames remain unknown. There is no computed ray, diffraction cone,
  propagated field section, device LUT or instrumental noise model.
- Four old modes and v1/v2 settings formats remain closed. A has no settings
  export, MAT/reference import, paper residual, fitting, sweep, result archive,
  database, hardware connection or deployment. No third-party source/image/data
  is embedded. The unsent author-question draft remains unchanged.
- Global16MiB persistent/32MiB transient budgets cover declared app-owned
  scientific/display/transport/document/preparation/rollback owners, not whole
  browser heap, process memory, framebuffer or GPU-driver allocation. Release
  does not require synchronous garbage collection or cancel a backend worker.
- The existing service remains loopback127.0.0.1:8510. No unrelated service or
  port8501 is managed. Browser/renderer availability is a real acceptance gate.
- The Windows symlink privilege skip is retained and reported; no elevation
  removes it. The historical Chrome response-capture failure is not rewritten
  as resolved. Actual current runs and any setup/failure evidence stay distinct.
- **B/Figure2a experimental reproduction remains unestablished.** Missing
  uncertainty does not prohibit a future descriptive comparison once its actual
  observable/axis/estimator mappings are established; uncertainty-qualified
  experimental validation is a separate gate.
- Teaching is separate; no learning-record or lesson-completion claim is made.
